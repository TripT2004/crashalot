## Candidate found in the bounded review

[ICAO Safety Report, 2025 edition](https://www.icao.int/sites/default/files/sp-files/safety/Documents/ICAO_SR_2025.pdf), pages 6–10 and 20, publishes matched indicators for scheduled commercial fixed-wing operations involving aircraft with certified maximum take-off weight above 5,700 kg. The report describes ICAO-validated accident records and ICAO Air Transport Bureau departure data. Pages 7 and 10 show annual departures and fatal accidents for 2019–2024. The 2024 overview reports 37.09 million departures and 10 fatal accidents. Departure figures are published rounded, unlike the exact NTSB counts previously animated.

This is a credible worldwide rate source when its own accident numerator is retained. Its aircraft threshold, accident taxonomy and review process differ from the PlaneCrashData source category. Worldwide geography alone does not establish identical populations. The full historical archive therefore still requires reconciliation or an explicit distinction in measurement; no claim is made that ICAO and the archive match record for record.

A second quick check found [IATA's 2025 report release](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2026-releases/2026-03-09-01/) with worldwide flights and accidents, but different inclusion/classification rules and revisions to earlier figures. Do not mix editions or organizations to manufacture one time series. ICAO is the cleaner candidate for a scheduled-operations framing.
